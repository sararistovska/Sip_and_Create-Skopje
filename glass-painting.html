About the workshop

A glass is your new canvas.

Welcome to an evening where creativity meets good company. During our Glass Painting workshop, you will transform a simple glass into something completely personal and unique.

Whether you prefer delicate flowers, colorful patterns, abstract shapes or something fun and unexpected, there are no rules here.

You don't need experience. Just come and bring your imagination.
