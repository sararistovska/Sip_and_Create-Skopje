Our workshops

Three ways to
spend an evening.

Glass Painting

Design and personalize two glasses and one candle under the guidance of our skilled instructors. All materials like glasses & candles, brushes, paints, and templates are included.

Pasta Making

Learn how to craft delicious homemade pasta from scratch under the guidance of our chefs, and enjoy tasting your creations.

Ceramics & Wine

Paint and decorate your own ceramic piece while enjoying a glass of fine wine under the guidance of our instructors.
