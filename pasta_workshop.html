The Workshop

The Recipe Card

Fresh pasta,
then you choose the shape.

The same guide we hand you at the table. Every guest rolls the same dough — then the road splits: tagliatelle one way, ravioli the other.

01
Step 1

Place the flour on a clean work surface, make a well in the middle and add the eggs.

02
Step 2

The general rule is 1 large egg to every 100g of flour.

03
Step 3

Using a fork, whisk the eggs while slowly incorporating the flour until combined, and it looks like a very rough dough.

04
Step 4

You may need to wet your hands once most of the egg is incorporated to help it along.

05
Step 5

Form а ball. Knead the dough for 10 minutes until smooth and pliable.

06
Step 6

Wrap the dough in cling film or cover with a towel and leave to rest for 30 minutes, out of the fridge.

07
Step 7

Divide the dough into softer portions, gently pressing and shaping each piece before rolling further.

08
Step 8

Run the dough through the pasta machine, notch by notch, until it's rolled thin into long, even sheets.

Two sheets. Two directions.

The long way.

Simple, silky ribbons made for twirling around your fork and finishing with your favourite sauce.

9a
Cutting

Dust the sheet with flour, fold it loosely, then slice it into ribbons.

9b
Drying

Nest the ribbons on a floured rack to dry.

Tagliatelle
Ready to plate

The filled way.

Delicate little pockets filled, sealed and ready to hold all the flavor inside.

9a
Filling

Spoon small mounds of filling across one sheet, spaced evenly apart.

9b
Shaping

Lay a second sheet on top, press out the air around each mound, then cut and seal the edges.
