GOOD TO KNOW
Frequently asked questions
Tap a card to flip it over.
What’s included in the workshop?
TAP
The workshop includes all materials, tools and professional guidance needed to create your own product.
Can I bring my own design?
TAP
Yes! You can bring your own design or follow one of our templates. The goal is to have fun and help you bring your creative vision to life.
What kind and how many drinks are included in the price?
TAP
The price includes unlimited drinks for the entire duration of the workshop. The main concept is based on wine, but we’ll soon introduce themed workshops featuring your favorite cocktails.
Can I organize a private event?
TAP
Absolutely! Sip & Create.Skopje is the perfect place for private events. Contact us for details on organizing your creative gathering.
What’s the price and duration of a workshop?
TAP
Each workshop lasts 2-3 hours and costs 1500 MKD, including drinks and all necessary materials
Can I keep my creation?
TAP
Yes! All participants can keep their creations and take them home as a memory from Sip & Create.Skopje.
