The Finish

One piece.
Two ways to glaze it.

Once your piece is painted and dry, you choose how it gets its final shine. Pick one, or glaze two pieces differently.

Option A

Glossy

A clear glaze that seals the colour and fires into a deep, reflective shine.

  1. Glazing

    Dip the piece or brush on an even coat of clear glaze, covering it completely so no raw clay shows.

  2. Firing

    In the kiln the glaze melts into a smooth, glassy layer that makes every colour underneath pop.

Option B

Matte

A soft, non-reflective glaze that keeps the texture of your brushwork visible.

  1. Glazing

    Brush on a thin, even layer of matte glaze, taking care not to let it pool in the grooves.

  2. Firing

    The kiln sets it into a smooth, soft-touch surface with almost no shine.

Either way, your piece leaves the table ready for its final firing.

After the Kiln

Painted by you.
Yours to keep.

You don't take it home the same night. A painted piece still needs glaze and a kiln before it's finished. That part is on us.

You finish your piece and leave it with us, labelled with your name.

The next days We glaze and fire

A clear glaze and a kiln firing make the colours glossy and food-safe.

About a week later You pick it up

We let you know when it's ready, then it's yours to use every day.
